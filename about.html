Where Healthy Hair
Meets Quiet Luxury

Lumière began with a simple belief: beautiful hair starts with healthy hair. What started as a two-chair studio has grown into a warm, light-filled salon where every guest is treated like family.

Our stylists combine years of training with a genuine love for their craft. We listen first, then create looks that feel effortless, flattering and truly you — using only premium, gentle products.
